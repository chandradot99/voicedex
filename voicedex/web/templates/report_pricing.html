How this is calculated

  • Source. Each price is read from the provider's own pricing page or API and stored with its source link and the date it was checked; every model page shows both. The catalog is versioned in git, so every price change has a history.
  • Which price. The published list price anyone can pay without a subscription commitment. Plan-gated rates, volume discounts and free tiers are recorded on each model page but not used here.
  • What's counted. Active models from providers that are still selling, with each -latest alias counted once. Prices are normalized to USD per 1M characters for text-to-speech and USD per hour of audio for speech-to-text and speech-to-speech; prices in other currencies or units are counted in the last table but not compared.
  • Ranges, not extremes. Spreads use the 5th to 95th percentile, so one unusual price at either end can't distort them. Single cheapest models change often and some depend on conditions, so they are on the price-sorted pages, with notes, rather than here.
  • Streaming means the model accepts or returns audio over WebSocket or WebRTC.
